# Research screening register

Search date: 29 October 2026. This is a rapid product-evidence map, not a systematic review or clinical validation.

100 unique records screened: 15 product-relevant candidates, 23 context-only records and 62 excluded from product-rule evidence.

Six Europe PMC searches retrieved up to 100 records each. Candidates were deduplicated by PMID, prioritised by publication-type metadata and screened using titles plus opening/concluding abstract sentences. The remaining abstract, methods, risk of bias, funding, overlapping cohorts and editorial peer-review status have not been individually audited for these 100 records. Metadata is not proof of peer review. A paper count is not a quality score.

The broad search surfaced substantial clinical and molecular research outside DietPal's general adult planning scope. Those records are retained transparently rather than silently counted as supporting evidence. Key older trials and reviews were also found by focused primary-source searches and are discussed in the planning rationale.

## Reproducible search topics

- personalised: `(TITLE_ABS:"personalized nutrition" OR TITLE_ABS:"personalised nutrition" OR TITLE_ABS:"tailored dietary")`
- planning: `(TITLE_ABS:"meal planning" OR TITLE_ABS:"menu planning" OR TITLE_ABS:"diet optimization")`
- meal_pattern: `(TITLE_ABS:"meal frequency" OR TITLE_ABS:"meal timing" OR TITLE_ABS:"time restricted eating")`
- protein_macros: `(TITLE_ABS:"protein distribution" OR TITLE_ABS:"high protein diet" OR TITLE_ABS:"macronutrient composition")`
- plant_diets: `(TITLE_ABS:"vegetarian diet" OR TITLE_ABS:"vegan diet" OR TITLE_ABS:"dietary diversity")`
- app_adherence: `(TITLE_ABS:"dietary self-monitoring" OR TITLE_ABS:"nutrition app" OR TITLE_ABS:"dietary adherence")`

All topics added `SRC:MED AND HAS_ABSTRACT:Y AND FIRST_PDATE:[2010-01-01 TO 2026-10-29]`, excluding Editorial, Letter, Comment and Preprint publication types. Protocols, duplicates, missing abstracts/PMIDs, retractions flagged by the service and selected out-of-scope title terms were excluded before the 100-record screen. Selection was capped by topic; it is not exhaustive or independent dual review. Raw responses and complete abstracts remain in the private review directory. Only bibliographic metadata and original screening notes are included here.
